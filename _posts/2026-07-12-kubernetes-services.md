**Table 1. IPv4 Private Ranges (RFC 1918)** 

| Class | IP Range | Description 
| :--- | :--- | :--- 
| A | 10.0.0.0 – 10.255.255.255 | Provides over 16 million addresses; used in large enterprise and corporate networks. |
| B | 172.16.0.0 – 172.31.255.255 | Provides about 1 million addresses; used in medium-sized organizations or universities. |
| C | 192.168.0.0 – 192.168.255.255 | Provides 65,536 addresses; most common for home routers and small offices. |
